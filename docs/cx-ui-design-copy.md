## Voice rules (read before editing anything)

1. **Write it the way you'd say it to a friend who runs a business.** If a
   12-year-old wouldn't get the sentence, rewrite it.
2. **One idea per sentence.** Most sentences should be under 15 words.
3. **Name the customer's problem before naming the service.**
4. **Real numbers only.** Every result on this page is from a published UMM
   case study. Never round up, never invent, never add one without a source.
5. **Words we don't use:** leverage, holistic, seamless, synergy, robust,
   omnichannel, touchpoint, stakeholder, ecosystem, empower, utilise,
   end-to-end (say "start to finish"), optimise (say "improve"),
   transformation, best-in-class, cutting-edge, world-class.
6. Chips are the small tilted pill tags that sit on each section headline.
   They can be playful; headlines must be clear first and clever second.

## 6. How we design

**Chip:** `Our rules`

**Headline**
> Good design isn't luck.
> It's rules we *never skip.*

**Lead**
> Every screen we make follows a few simple rules. That's why it looks good
> and works.

| Rule | Line |
|---|---|
| **Colour with a reason** | Colours that guide the eye, show what to tap, and stay readable for everyone, including people with low vision. |
| **Made for thumbs** | Buttons where your thumb naturally lands, and text big enough to read on the go. |
| **Fewer steps, every time** | If it takes ten taps, we find a way to do it in three. |
| **Same look, everywhere** | One set of buttons, colours and patterns across web and app, so nothing ever feels unfamiliar. |
| **Tested before it's built** | We check designs with real users and your developers *during* design, not after. Quality is checked as we build, and we agree how success is measured after launch. |

**Chip:** `How it works`

### Questions (accordion beside the closing button)

**What happens in a CX check-up?**
We study a few of your most important customer journeys, look at how they
work today, and agree what success looks like. You get a ranked plan of what
to fix first, and a research report. Before we start, we confirm the scope,
what we need from your team, and the time and cost.

**Can we start with just one journey?**
Yes, and lots of clients do. We map, design and test one journey from start
to finish. It's the quickest way to see results before doing more.

**Do you only design, or build too?**
Both. We can design and hand over to your team, design and build it
ourselves, or work alongside your developers. We agree which at the start,
so everyone knows who does what.

**Can you work with our in-house team?**
Yes. We often work inside client teams, using your tools and ways of
working. When we leave, your team can run it themselves.

**What access or data do you need?**
Usually a small group of customers to talk to, and access to the data for
the journeys we're working on. We agree exactly what's needed, including
privacy rules, before we start.

**How will we know it's working?**
We agree the numbers up front, like sales, repeat customers, support calls
or how much effort things take. Then we record where they are today, so we
can show you the change.

**Will this disrupt our business?**
No. We roll changes out in stages, not all at once, and plan around anything
that has to keep running.
